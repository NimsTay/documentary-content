1. Taylor Swift: The Snake That Built a Stadium | From Cancellation to Domination


===== DESCRIPTION =====
Millions called Taylor Swift a liar and a snake and said her career was over. Seven years later, her fans shook the ground like an earthquake.

In 2015, Taylor Swift was on top of the world with 1989. Then came the summer it all broke: Kanye West's song Famous, Kim Kardashian's recorded phone call clips, a flood of snake emojis, and a hashtag declaring her career over. So Taylor did something nobody expected. She disappeared.

This documentary follows how Taylor Swift came back. She turned the snake into the symbol of the Reputation era, fought for her master recordings by re-recording her albums as Taylor's Version, and won Album of the Year with Folklore.

Then came the Eras Tour. Ticketmaster crashed, senators held a hearing, and the crowd in Seattle set off sensors like a 2.3 magnitude quake. It became the first tour ever to pass one billion dollars, and it ended at more than two billion.

How did one woman turn the internet's worst insult into the biggest tour in history?
