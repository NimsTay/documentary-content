GTA 6: The Longest Wait In Gaming | Was it Worth the Wait?

===== DESCRIPTION =====
GTA 6 has been 13 years in the making. GTA V made 800 million dollars in a single day in 2013, and the next one was supposed to be right around the corner.

This documentary follows the story of Grand Theft Auto, from a small studio in Scotland in 1997 to GTA III, Vice City and San Andreas. Then the rhythm stopped. GTA Online turned into a money machine, Red Dead Redemption 2 took Rockstar's best minds, and the studio went silent for years.

Then things went wrong. Co-founder and head writer Dan Houser left in 2020. In 2022 a hacker leaked 90 videos of the unfinished game. When the GTA 6 trailer finally arrived in December 2023, it leaked early too, and still hit 90 million views in 24 hours. It showed Vice City, the state of Leonida, and two criminals named Lucia and Jason.

Then came the delays: from 2025 to May 2026, and then to November 2026. With a budget analysts believe could top one billion dollars, Rockstar is betting everything on getting it right.

Could any game live up to 13 years of dreams?
